Introduction

Why parking has become a community-wide problem.

🚗

Growing Vehicles

Rapid growth in vehicles has made parking a major challenge at hospitals, colleges, markets and bus stations.

⏱️

Wasted Time

Drivers waste time searching for vacant slots, adding to traffic congestion, fuel wastage and air pollution.

🤖

Our Proposal

An AI-Based Smart Parking Management System that guides drivers to available spaces in real time.

Objectives & Goals

What this project set out to achieve.

1

Study existing parking management challenges in public places and their impact on the community.

2

Analyze public awareness of smart parking technologies and the need for automated parking systems.

3

Develop an AI-Based Smart Parking Management System that detects available spaces in real time.

4

Reduce traffic congestion, fuel consumption and time spent searching for parking.

Methodology

How we carried out the study, from planning to analysis.

1

Requirement gathering: identified problems and the public places to study in Mylavaram.

2

Survey design: prepared a structured questionnaire (demographics, parking problems, awareness, need for a smart system).

3

Field visits: observed parking conditions first-hand at hospitals, colleges, markets and bus stations.

4

Data collection & analysis: collected 16 responses and analysed them into charts.

5

Literature & prototype study: reviewed existing smart parking approaches to shape our proposal.

➜
🧠

Conclusion

  • Limited availability, time wastage and congestion create significant inconvenience for vehicle users in Mylavaram.
  • The study showed strong community interest in a Smart Parking System with real-time availability and guidance.
  • With continued development, these findings can guide deployment of an AI-based, community-friendly parking solution.
